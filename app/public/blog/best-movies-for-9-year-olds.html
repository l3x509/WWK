Best Movies for 9 Year Olds in 2026

🍿 WatchWithKids July 27, 2026 8 min read

Nine-year-olds want to be taken seriously. They can follow layered plots, spot real wit, and sit with genuine emotional stakes — but they still light up at pure adventure and spectacle. These 13 films hit that balance precisely.

⚡ Adventure — real stakes

Nine-year-olds are ready for genuine tension and consequence. These deliver excitement that doesn't feel babyish, without tipping into anything too intense.

🐺
Wolfwalkers (2020)
Ages 7–12103 minApple TV+PG

A girl in 17th-century Ireland befriends a wild child who transforms into a wolf. Cartoon Saloon's hand-painted visuals are unlike anything else in mainstream animation, and the story tackles colonialism and freedom in ways nine-year-olds are genuinely ready to engage with.

⭐ Parent note: Possibly the most visually stunning animated film of the last decade.
🕷️
Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse (2018)
Ages 7–12117 minNetflixPG

A Brooklyn teenager discovers he's not the only Spider-Man in the multiverse. Genuinely groundbreaking animation paired with a story about identity and self-belief that lands with real weight at this age.

⭐ Parent note: Regularly cited as one of the best films of the decade, not just among kids' films.
🌙
Kubo and the Two Strings (2016)
Ages 8–12101 minAnyPG

A boy with magical storytelling powers goes on a quest to find his father's armor. Laika's stop-motion craft is extraordinary, and the story deals honestly with grief and family without becoming overwhelming.

⭐ Parent note: Some genuinely eerie imagery — know your kid's tolerance for spooky before pressing play.
🗺️
The Mitchells vs. the Machines (2021)
Ages 6–12114 minNetflixPG

A chaotic family road trip gets interrupted by a robot uprising. Fast, funny, and inventive, with a father-daughter dynamic that nine-year-olds — often navigating their own independence — connect with more than younger viewers do.

⭐ Parent note: One of the funniest family films of the last several years, full stop.

😄 Funny — sharp comedy

Nine-year-olds have real comic taste by this point — they want cleverness, not just noise. These reward it.

🧱
The LEGO Movie (2014)
Ages 5–12100 minAnyPG

An ordinary minifig is mistakenly identified as the key to saving the universe. Relentlessly funny at every level, with a third-act twist that lands especially well for kids old enough to think about what stories are actually for.

⭐ Parent note: The ending is aimed squarely at the adults in the room — it's a real gut-punch.
🐻
Paddington 2 (2017)
Ages 4–12103 minNetflixPG

Often cited as one of the best-reviewed films of its decade for any audience. Warm, sharply funny, and anchored by a genuinely great comic villain performance from Hugh Grant.

⭐ Parent note: If you watch one film on this list with the whole family, make it this one.
📚
Matilda the Musical (2022)
Ages 5–12117 minNetflixPG

A brilliant girl with terrible parents discovers she has extraordinary powers. The songs are excellent and the story champions independent thinking in a way nine-year-olds — increasingly asserting their own opinions — respond to strongly.

⭐ Parent note: Emma Thompson's Miss Trunchbull is a genuinely great villain performance.

❤️ Emotional — the ones that stick

Nine is an age when films start leaving a real mark. These prompt conversations worth having afterward.

🏮
Spirited Away (2001)
Ages 8–12125 minMaxPG

A girl's parents are turned into pigs and she must navigate a spirit world to save them. Widely regarded as one of the greatest animated films ever made, and nine is an excellent age for a first viewing — complex enough to reward close attention, strange enough to be unforgettable.

⭐ Parent note: Watch it together rather than putting it on and leaving the room — it rewards discussion.
🎺
Coco (2017)
Ages 5–12105 minDisney+PG

A boy journeys to the Land of the Dead to uncover his family's hidden history. Pixar's most emotionally ambitious film, dealing with memory, death, and legacy through stunning visuals and music.

⭐ Parent note: The final act is genuinely moving for kids and adults both. Keep tissues nearby.
🎈
Up (2009)
Ages 7–1296 minDisney+PG

An elderly widower ties thousands of balloons to his house to fulfill a lifelong dream. The opening montage is one of the most quietly devastating sequences in animated film — nine-year-olds can hold its weight, and it makes the adventure that follows land harder.

⭐ Parent note: The opening ten minutes are genuinely sad. The rest of the film earns its lightness because of it.
🐲
How to Train Your Dragon (2010)
Ages 5–1298 minNetflixPG

A young Viking who can't fight befriends the most feared dragon in the world. A story about empathy and difference that rewards rewatching at this age — kids notice more of its subtlety than they did a few years earlier.

⭐ Parent note: The score alone is worth the runtime. Holds up beautifully.

Tips for movie night with a 9-year-old

Let them weigh in on tone, not just title. Nine-year-olds often have real opinions about whether they want something funny, sad, or thrilling tonight. Asking "adventure or something that'll make us cry?" respects that they know their own mood.

Don't shy away from real sadness. Films like Up and Coco handle loss with honesty rather than avoidance, and nine-year-olds are well-equipped to sit with that — often better than parents expect.

Match runtime to your night. Spirited Away (125 min) is a weekend film. Up (96 min) and How to Train Your Dragon (98 min) fit a school night comfortably.

Watch Spirited Away together, not as background. It's dense and rewards active attention — treat it as an event rather than a wind-down.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best movie for a 9-year-old?

Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse and Wolfwalkers are the strongest adventure picks for this age right now, both praised for exceptional animation and genuine storytelling. For something more emotional, Coco and Up are the standouts.

Can a 9-year-old watch PG-13 movies?

Many nine-year-olds are ready for select PG-13 content, but the 13 films on this list — all G or PG — already offer real depth, humor, and emotional stakes without needing to reach for an older rating. Individual readiness varies significantly.

What are the best emotional movies for a 9-year-old?

Coco, Up, and Spirited Away all deal honestly with loss and memory in ways nine-year-olds are well-equipped to process, and all three tend to prompt meaningful conversation afterward.

How long should a movie be for a 9-year-old?

Most nine-year-olds can comfortably handle films up to around 120 minutes, especially on weekends. For school nights, aim for under 105 minutes to leave room for winding down before bed.
