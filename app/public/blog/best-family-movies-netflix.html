Best Family Movies on Netflix Right Now

UPDATED OCT 2026 · 6 MIN READ · WATCHWITHKIDS

Netflix has thousands of kids' titles and an algorithm designed to surface whatever's cheapest to license. This guide cuts through it. Here are 12 picks (11 films and one series) actually worth a Friday night — sorted by age and mood so you can find the right one in under a minute.

Netflix's catalog rotates every month, and titles come and go without warning, so we can't promise every pick here is on Netflix today. Netflix originals (Klaus, Matilda the Musical, The Mitchells vs. the Machines, The Sea Beast, The Dragon Prince) stay put; licensed films like Paddington 2 and How to Train Your Dragon move around. 🧒 Ages 3–6: Short and gentle

For this age group you want films around 100 minutes or shorter, with low peril and clear storytelling. These deliver.

🐑

Shaun the Sheep Movie (2015)

Ages 3–10 85 min G

The beloved Aardman sheep leads his flock to the big city. Wordless slapstick at its absolute finest — every age in the room will find something to laugh at, and the pacing is perfect for young children.

⭐ Parent note: Completely wordless — great for multilingual families and adults who need a break from explanation duty.

🎄

Klaus (2019)

Ages 4–10 96 min PG

Netflix's own hand-drawn animated film about a selfish postman who accidentally creates the legend of Santa Claus. Stunningly gorgeous and surprisingly moving. The best original animated film Netflix has made.

⭐ Parent note: You will absolutely cry at the end. This is a guarantee, not a warning.

🐉

How to Train Your Dragon (2010)

Ages 5–12 98 min PG

A Viking boy befriends the most feared dragon in the world. One of the most rewatchable family films ever made — the flying sequences are genuinely breathtaking and the story about empathy and difference is timeless.

⭐ Parent note: Still holds up completely. If you haven't seen it as a parent, prepare to see it differently.

👦 Ages 6–9: Ready for a real story

This age group can handle longer films with proper narrative arcs, mild peril, and real emotional stakes.

🤖

The Mitchells vs. the Machines (2021)

Ages 6–12 114 min PG

A chaotic, hilarious family road trip gets interrupted by a robot apocalypse. Produced by the Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse team — the animation style is inventive and the parent-teen dynamic is surprisingly honest.

⭐ Parent note: Secretly a film about a father learning to understand his daughter. The ending lands beautifully.

🦁

Puss in Boots: The Last Wish (2022)

Ages 6–12 100 min PG

The best animated film of recent years, full stop. A cat facing the end of his nine lives goes on a quest for a wishing star. The animation is revolutionary and the villain — Death as a wolf — is genuinely iconic.

⭐ Parent note: Don't be put off by it being a Shrek spin-off. This is a masterpiece. Adults rate it higher than the kids.

🧱

The LEGO Movie (2014)

Ages 5–12 100 min PG

An ordinary LEGO minifig is mistakenly identified as the most extraordinary person and key to saving the world. A film that works on every level — slapstick for young kids, sharp satire for adults, and a third-act reveal that nobody sees coming.

⭐ Parent note: "Everything is Awesome" will live in your head for weeks. You've been warned.

📚

Matilda the Musical (2022)

Ages 5–12 117 min PG

Roald Dahl's story of a brilliant girl with terrible parents, reimagined as a bold musical. The songs are excellent, the performances are extraordinary, and it champions reading and independent thinking in a way that few kids' films manage.

⭐ Parent note: Emma Thompson as Miss Trunchbull is one of the great villain performances. Extraordinary casting.

🧑 Ages 8–12: Something with real depth

Older kids can handle moral ambiguity, complex characters, and longer runtimes. These deliver on all fronts.

🕷️

Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse (2018)

Ages 7–12 117 min PG

A Brooklyn teenager discovers he's not the only Spider-Man in the multiverse. The animation broke new ground and still looks unlike anything else made before or since. A story about identity, belonging, and living up to your potential.

⭐ Parent note: One of the best superhero films ever made, full stop — not just for kids.

🐲

The Dragon Prince (Netflix Original series)

Ages 8–12 Series TV-Y7

From a former head writer of Avatar: The Last Airbender, this epic fantasy animated series tackles war, prejudice, and sacrifice with more depth than most adult dramas. If your older kids haven't discovered this yet, a rainy weekend is the perfect time to start.

⭐ Parent note: Introduces themes of grief, political complexity, and moral grey areas — all handled beautifully.

🌊

The Sea Beast (2022)

Ages 7–12 115 min PG

A Netflix original about a monster hunter and a girl stowaway who discover the beasts they hunt aren't what everyone says. Big sea-monster action, a lovable creature, and a real message about questioning what you've been taught.

⭐ Parent note: There's real peril and some intense sea battles, so it suits confident 7 and 8 year olds better than sensitive ones.

All ages: the ones that work for everyone

Sometimes you have an 8-year-old and a 4-year-old and you just need something that works for both. These do.

🐻

Paddington 2 (2017)

Ages 4–12 103 min PG

Often cited as one of the best films of its decade — for any age. Warm, funny, and surprisingly moving, with a performance by Hugh Grant that's a genuine comedic masterwork. If you watch one film on this list, make it this one.

⭐ Parent note: Critics gave this a 99% on Rotten Tomatoes. It deserves every point.

🦔

Over the Hedge (2006)

Ages 5–12 83 min PG

Suburban wildlife waking up to discover a housing development in their forest. Funnier than you remember, sharper than it looks, and one of the most underrated family comedies of the 2000s. At 83 minutes it's one of the rare films that doesn't overstay its welcome.

⭐ Parent note: The caffeine-addicted squirrel is one of animated cinema's great characters.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best family movie on Netflix right now?

For most families, Paddington 2 is the single best choice — it works for ages 4 through adult and has a near-perfect critical reception. For families with older kids, Puss in Boots: The Last Wish is the more adventurous pick. Use WatchWithKids to find options specific to your kids' ages.

Are there good Netflix movies for mixed age groups?

Paddington 2, Klaus, Over the Hedge, and Shaun the Sheep Movie all work well for mixed-age groups where you have younger and older kids watching together. These films have enough depth for older viewers while remaining accessible and gentle for young ones.

What Netflix family movies are good for parents too?

Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse, Puss in Boots: The Last Wish, The Mitchells vs. the Machines, and Paddington 2 are all highly rated by adult critics and genuinely entertaining for parents to watch. Enable Parent Enjoyment Mode on WatchWithKids to filter for these specifically.
