Best Family Movies on Amazon Prime Video

🍿 WatchWithKids July 27, 2026 7 min read

Amazon Prime's kids section leans harder into TV than movies, and the catalog can feel like a mix of true gems and forgettable filler. Here are 13 family films on Prime Video actually worth your evening, sorted by age and mood.

In this guide
  • 🧒 Ages 4–8: warm and accessible
  • 👦 Ages 7–12: more story, more stakes
  • 🍿 All ages: the ones that work for everyone
  • Frequently asked questions

Availability on Prime Video shifts often, since Amazon's licensing deals rotate titles in and out more frequently than some competitors. Use WatchWithKids to confirm what's live right now — we pull current data directly.

🧒 Ages 4–8: warm and accessible

🕸️
Charlotte's Web (2006)
Ages 4–1097 minPrime VideoG

A pig named Wilbur is saved from the dinner table by a resourceful spider named Charlotte. A gentle, beautifully told classic about friendship and sacrifice — reliably one of the best-reviewed family films on the platform.

⭐ Parent note: A genuine tearjerker at the end, in the best way. Keep tissues nearby.
🐻
Paddington (2014)
Ages 4–1095 minPrime VideoPG

A young bear from Peru is taken in by a skeptical London family. Warm, funny, and beautifully made — Paddington's earnest goodness wins over everyone in the room, on screen and off.

⭐ Parent note: One of the most purely likeable films ever made for this age group.
🍫
Hotel Transylvania (2012)
Ages 5–1191 minPrime VideoPG

Dracula runs a hotel where monsters can relax away from humans — until one wanders in. Colorful, fast-paced, and consistently a hit with younger kids who enjoy monsters that are silly rather than scary.

⭐ Parent note: Loud and high-energy — a good pick when your kids need to burn off some steam.

👦 Ages 7–12: more story, more stakes

🌱
The Croods (2013)
Ages 6–1298 minPrime VideoPG

A prehistoric family ventures beyond their sheltered cave for the first time when their home is destroyed. Genuinely funny, visually inventive, and built around a warm father-daughter dynamic that resonates well with this age group.

⭐ Parent note: Better than its trailers suggest — a real emotional core beneath the slapstick.
👻
IF (2024)
Ages 7–12104 minPrime VideoPG

A girl discovers she can see other people's forgotten imaginary friends and sets out to reconnect them with their kids. A blend of live action and animation from John Krasinski, with real emotional depth beneath its whimsical premise.

⭐ Parent note: Some genuinely moving moments about growing up and letting go — worth watching together.
🌌
Zathura: A Space Adventure (2005)
Ages 7–12101 minPrime VideoPG

Two feuding brothers are launched into a real space adventure by a mysterious board game. Genuine peril and real sibling stakes make this land harder — and more satisfyingly — than a typical adventure film at this age.

⭐ Parent note: A handful of moments may be intense for sensitive kids on the younger end of this range.

🍿 All ages: the ones that work for everyone

🐔
Chicken Run (2000)
Ages 5–1284 minPrime VideoG

A group of chickens plots an escape from their farm before they end up as pot pies. Aardman's stop-motion charm and sly wit make this one of the rare films that genuinely holds every age in the room at once.

⭐ Parent note: The comic timing is impeccable — adults tend to enjoy this every bit as much as kids do.
👽
E.T. the Extra-Terrestrial (1982)
Ages 7–12115 minPrime VideoPG

A lonely boy befriends a stranded alien and helps him find a way home. A near-perfect encapsulation of childhood — kids are captivated by ET himself, adults get the full weight of Spielberg's nostalgia and craft.

⭐ Parent note: Expect real tears by the end. This one earns them honestly.
🎩
Matilda (1996)
Ages 6–1298 minPrime VideoPG

A brilliant girl with neglectful parents discovers she has telekinetic powers and uses them to stand up for herself and her classmates. Roald Dahl's story of righteous rebellion against unfair adults resonates at every age.

⭐ Parent note: Miss Trunchbull is genuinely one of cinema's great villains — memorable for kids and parents alike.
🕵️
Despicable Me (2010)
Ages 5–1295 minPrime VideoPG

A supervillain's plan to steal the moon is derailed when he adopts three orphan girls as part of a scheme — and falls for them for real. Funny, warm, and the film that launched the Minions into the cultural stratosphere.

⭐ Parent note: The emotional turn in the back half is more sincere than the trailers let on.
Quick reference — by age

Ages 4–8: Charlotte's Web, Paddington, Hotel Transylvania

Ages 7–12: The Croods, IF, Zathura

All ages together: Chicken Run, E.T., Matilda, Despicable Me

Frequently asked questions

What is the best family movie on Amazon Prime right now?

Paddington and Charlotte's Web are the strongest picks for younger kids, both beautifully made and consistently well-reviewed. For a film that works across the whole family, Chicken Run and E.T. are excellent choices.

Does Amazon Prime have good movies for kids?

Yes, though the selection leans more toward TV than movies compared to Netflix or Disney+. The films that are there include some genuine classics — Charlotte's Web, E.T., and Matilda among them — alongside newer titles like IF.

Are Amazon Prime family movies included free with Prime?

Titles available through Prime Video's standard library are included with a Prime membership. Some newer releases may be available only to rent or buy even for Prime members — check the listing before assuming a title is free to stream.

What are good Amazon Prime movies for a wide age range?

Chicken Run, E.T., Matilda, and Despicable Me all work well across a broad age range, from around five through twelve, making them solid picks for mixed-age family movie nights.
