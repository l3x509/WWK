Best Halloween Movies for Kids, by Scare Level

🍿 WatchWithKids Updated October 11, 2026 6 min read

The hard part of Halloween movie night isn't finding a spooky movie. It's finding one that fits your kid's actual tolerance for scary. This guide sorts 13 classic Halloween films into three scare levels, so you can pick the right one for a 4 year old, a 7 year old or an 11 year old.

Quick answer

Ages 3–5: It's the Great Pumpkin, Charlie Brown or Hotel Transylvania. Ages 6–7: Casper, Hocus Pocus or The Addams Family. Ages 8 and up: The Nightmare Before Christmas, Coraline or Goosebumps. Age notes are our own judgment from watching these films. Your child's temperament matters more than the age on the box.

Level 1: Cozy, almost no scares (ages 3+)

These have the Halloween look without the fear. They are also the right pick when you have a mix of ages in the room.

It's the Great Pumpkin, Charlie Brown (1966)
TV-G · about 25 min

The shortest and gentlest option on this page. Nothing frightening happens, and the whole thing is over before bedtime. A good first Halloween watch for ages 3 and up.

Hotel Transylvania (2012)
PG · 91 min

Monsters run a hotel and a human walks in. It's slapstick and silliness rather than scares, which makes it the easy choice for ages 4 and up.

Wallace & Gromit: The Curse of the Were-Rabbit (2005)
G · about 85 min

A monster-movie spoof in clay. The creature is more funny than frightening, and the jokes land for parents too. Good for ages 5 and up.

Casper (1995)
PG · 100 min

A friendly ghost, with some sad moments about loss. A few ghost-trio scenes are mildly spooky. Most kids 5 and up are fine; sensitive ones may want a hand to hold.

Level 2: Spooky fun (ages 6+)

Villains and monsters show up, but the tone stays playful. These are good for kids who like a little thrill and for families with a mix of ages 6 to 10.

Hocus Pocus (1993)
PG · 96 min

Three witches come back to life on Halloween. The villains are comic but menacing, and there's some mild adult humor that sails over most kids' heads. Works for ages 6 and up.

The Addams Family (2019)
PG · 87 min

Spooky aesthetic, dark jokes, and very little real fear. A decent step up from Hotel Transylvania for ages 6 and up.

Goosebumps (2015)
PG · 103 min

Monsters escape from their books. It's a live-action adventure with real jump moments, so it suits ages 8 and up better than younger kids.

Frankenweenie (2012)
PG · 87 min

Tim Burton's black-and-white story about a boy and his dog. It's lovely, but a pet dies early on, so preview it if your child is sensitive to animal loss. Ages 8 and up.

Level 3: Properly spooky (ages 8+)

These have real atmosphere and some genuinely creepy moments. Preview them, or watch together with the lights on, if your child is sensitive.

The Nightmare Before Christmas (1993)
PG · 76 min

Beautiful stop-motion, catchy songs and some creepy imagery (skeletons, a boogeyman). Many 7 and 8 year olds love it; some under 7 find parts unsettling.

Coraline (2009)
PG · 100 min

The scariest film on this page for most kids. The Other Mother and the button eyes stay with children. Best from about 8 and up, and preview it for sensitive kids.

Monster House (2006)
PG · 91 min

A haunted house that really does come alive. It's genuinely intense, with a real sense of threat. Best for ages 9 and up, or brave 8 year olds.

ParaNorman (2012)
PG · 92 min

A boy who talks to the dead faces a zombie curse. Funny and kind-hearted, but with scary zombie scenes and mature themes. Ages 9 and up.

Corpse Bride (2005)
PG · 77 min

Gothic and musical, with skeletons and a dark premise. It's gentler than it looks, but the tone is eerie. Ages 8 and up.

Tips for a good Halloween movie night

  • Start one level lower than you think. It's easy to go up a level next time and hard to un-scare a child.
  • Watch in the afternoon or early evening. Spooky films hit harder right before bed.
  • Keep the pause button close. A quick "that's just a puppet / animation" can reset a worried kid.
  • Know the sad moments, not just the scary ones. Casper and Frankenweenie both involve loss.
  • Mixed ages? Choose for the youngest child in the room. Our guide to what to watch when your kids are different ages has more ideas.

FAQ

What are the best Halloween movies for toddlers and preschoolers?

Stick to the shortest, gentlest options: It's the Great Pumpkin, Charlie Brown (about 25 minutes) and Hotel Transylvania for ages 4 and up. Avoid anything with a haunted-house premise until about age 6 or 7.

What are the best not-too-scary Halloween movies for 6 and 7 year olds?

Hotel Transylvania, Wallace & Gromit: The Curse of the Were-Rabbit, Casper and Hocus Pocus are the usual starting points. Hocus Pocus has some mild adult humor, and Casper has a few sad moments, so know your child.

Is Coraline okay for a 7 year old?

It's rated PG, but it's the scariest film on this list. Many parents wait until about 8 or older and preview it for kids who get frightened easily.

Is The Nightmare Before Christmas too scary for kids?

It's rated PG and many 7 and 8 year olds love it, but it has creepy imagery. Younger children and sensitive kids may find the boogeyman and skeleton scenes unsettling.
