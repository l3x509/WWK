Best Movies for 10 Year Olds in 2026

🍿 WatchWithKids July 27, 2026 8 min read

Ten is the edge of tween territory. Kids this age want real stakes, real wit, and stories that don't talk down to them — but they're still young enough to be swept away by pure spectacle. These 13 films hit that balance.

⚡ Adventure — big stakes

Ten-year-olds are ready for real tension and genuine consequence. These deliver excitement that respects their growing sophistication.

🕷️
Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse (2018)
Ages 7–12117 minNetflixPG

A Brooklyn teenager discovers he's not the only Spider-Man in the multiverse. Groundbreaking animation paired with a genuine coming-of-age story about stepping up when it's your turn — the kind of theme that lands hard at ten.

⭐ Parent note: Widely considered one of the best films of its decade, full stop.
🐺
Wolfwalkers (2020)
Ages 7–12103 minApple TV+PG

A girl in 17th-century Ireland befriends a wild child who transforms into a wolf. Hand-painted visuals unlike anything else in mainstream animation, with real ideas about colonialism and freedom that ten-year-olds engage with more directly than younger kids.

⭐ Parent note: Possibly the most visually stunning animated film of the last decade.
🏹
How to Train Your Dragon (2010)
Ages 5–1298 minNetflixPG

A young Viking who can't fight befriends the most feared dragon in the world. A rewatch at ten reveals more of its subtlety about empathy and proving yourself on your own terms than earlier viewings did.

⭐ Parent note: The score alone is worth the runtime. One of the great family scores.

😄 Funny — sharp wit

Ten-year-olds have real comic taste — they want cleverness and timing, not just noise.

🤖
The Mitchells vs. the Machines (2021)
Ages 6–12114 minNetflixPG

A chaotic family road trip gets hijacked by a robot uprising. Fast, inventive, and genuinely funny — the father-daughter dynamic at its center resonates strongly with kids at the age where independence is starting to feel real.

⭐ Parent note: One of the funniest family films of the last several years.
🐻
Paddington 2 (2017)
Ages 4–12103 minNetflixPG

Often cited as one of the best-reviewed films of its decade for any audience. Warm, sharply funny, and built around a genuinely great comic villain performance.

⭐ Parent note: If you watch one film on this list as a family, make it this one.
🦁
Puss in Boots: The Last Wish (2022)
Ages 6–12100 minNetflixPG

A legendary cat facing the end of his nine lives goes on one final quest. Genuinely revolutionary animation and a villain — Death, as a wolf — handled with real weight that ten-year-olds are ready for.

⭐ Parent note: The best animated film of recent years. Adults consistently rate it higher than kids do.

❤️ Emotional — the ones that stick

Ten is an age when films start leaving a real, lasting mark. These prompt the best conversations.

🏮
Spirited Away (2001)
Ages 8–12125 minMaxPG

A girl's parents are turned into pigs and she must navigate a spirit world to save them. Widely regarded as one of the greatest animated films ever made — ten is an excellent age for a first watch, old enough to sit with its strangeness and complexity.

⭐ Parent note: Watch it together, not as background. It rewards close attention and conversation after.
🎈
Up (2009)
Ages 7–1296 minDisney+PG

An elderly widower ties thousands of balloons to his house to fulfill a lifelong dream. The famous opening montage is genuinely moving, and ten-year-olds can hold its emotional weight — which makes the adventure that follows land with more meaning.

⭐ Parent note: The opening is sad. The rest of the film earns its lightness because of it.
🌊
Moana (2016)
Ages 4–10107 minDisney+PG

A chief's daughter sails beyond the reef to save her island. A story about identity and legacy that lands with real force for kids at this age, beginning to think seriously about who they want to become.

⭐ Parent note: Genuinely empowering, and the soundtrack holds up to endless replays.
🎺
Coco (2017)
Ages 5–12105 minDisney+PG

A boy journeys to the Land of the Dead to uncover his family's hidden history. Pixar's most emotionally ambitious film — ten-year-olds are old enough to sit with its themes of memory and legacy without needing much support.

⭐ Parent note: The final act is genuinely moving for kids and adults both. Keep tissues nearby.

Tips for movie night with a 10-year-old

Let them choose the tone. Ten-year-olds increasingly have strong opinions about what mood they're in. Asking directly — "adventure, funny, or something heavier?" — respects that they know what they want tonight.

Lean into complexity rather than avoiding it. Films like Spirited Away and Up handle real difficulty with honesty. Ten-year-olds are well-equipped for this, often more than parents expect.

Match runtime to the night. Spirited Away (125 min) is a weekend film. Up (96 min) and How to Train Your Dragon (98 min) fit a school night comfortably.

Watch the dense ones together. Spirited Away in particular rewards active attention and conversation afterward rather than being put on as background.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best movie for a 10-year-old?

Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse and Wolfwalkers are the strongest adventure picks right now, both praised for exceptional animation and real storytelling. For something more emotional, Coco and Up are the standouts.

Can a 10-year-old watch PG-13 movies?

Many ten-year-olds are ready for select PG-13 content, but the 13 films on this list — all PG — already offer real depth, humor, and stakes without needing an older rating. Readiness varies significantly by child.

What are the best emotional movies for a 10-year-old?

Spirited Away, Up, and Coco all deal honestly with loss, memory, or identity in ways ten-year-olds are well-equipped to process, and all three tend to prompt real conversation afterward.

How long should a movie be for a 10-year-old?

Most ten-year-olds can comfortably handle films up to around 120–125 minutes, especially on weekends. For school nights, aim for under 105 minutes.
