Christmas Movies for Kids, by Scare Level and Streaming Service

🍿 WatchWithKids Updated October 19, 2026 6 min read

Christmas movies are not all cozy. Some have ghosts, runaway trains and a missile or two. This guide sorts seven family favorites by how scary and sad they get, flags the animated ones, and shows where each streams, plus two new 2026 releases to watch for.

Quick answer

Ages 3 and up: A Charlie Brown Christmas. Ages 5 and up: The Grinch (2018) or The Muppet Christmas Carol. Ages 6 to 7 and up: Arthur Christmas, Klaus, The Polar Express or Elf. Age notes are our own judgment, so go by your child's temperament over the number.

Cozy pick (ages 3+)

A Charlie Brown Christmas (1965)
G · 25 min · Animated

The gentlest Christmas movie there is. Charlie Brown looks for the real point of the holiday, and nothing frightening happens. It is short enough for toddlers and a good first Christmas watch. Ages 3 and up.

Gentle, with a few shivers (ages 5+)

The Grinch (2018)
PG · 82 min · Animated

Mostly slapstick, with sled rides and falls. The tense moment is the Grinch nearly slipping off a mountain, and he is rescued. Expect a sad backstory explaining why he hates Christmas. Common Sense Media says 6 and up, and many parents say 5. Ages 5 and up.

The Muppet Christmas Carol (1992)
G · 85 min · Live action with puppets

A warm, funny version of the Dickens story. The Ghost of Christmas Future is the scary part, and in one possible future Tiny Tim dies. That can upset sensitive kids, so preview it if yours scare easily. Ages 5 and up.

More peril or edge (ages 6 to 7+)

Arthur Christmas (2011)
PG · 97 min · Animated

A clever, funny look at how Santa's operation really works. The danger is played for laughs, but a homeowner fires a gun at the characters and military missiles hit the sleigh. Ages 6 and up.

Klaus (2019)
PG · 96 min · Animated

A beautiful origin story for Santa. Two families keep a bitter feud going, with traps and chases, and one character is grieving a loss. It is moving, and more serious than the title suggests. Ages 6 to 7 and up.

The Polar Express (2004)
G · 100 min · Animated

Rated G, but not a gentle watch for every child. The train ride is fast and dangerous, and one scene has a puppet frighten a boy in a dark room of toys. Common Sense Media says 6 and up. Ages 6 to 7 and up.

Elf (2003)
PG · 95 min · Live action

Not scary, but it has mild swearing and crude jokes, which is why Common Sense Media says 8 and up and kids' reviews say 7. Park rangers chase Santa's sleigh at the end, played for laughs. Ages 7 and up.

Tips for a good Christmas movie night

  • Choose for the youngest child in the room. Our guide to what to watch when your kids are different ages can help.
  • Start with the short one. A Charlie Brown Christmas fits before dinner or bedtime.
  • Know the sad beats. The Muppet Christmas Carol and Klaus both have moments that land hard for sensitive kids.
  • Rent the day before, not a month early. Digital rentals do not run out, so there is no need to rush. But Prime Video rentals usually expire 30 days after you pay, and you get about 48 hours to finish once you press play. Other stores have their own windows, so check the rental page.

FAQ

What are the best Christmas movies for kids on Netflix?

Klaus (PG) is on Netflix as of October 19, 2026. It is a hand-drawn animated story with a feud between two families and a grieving character, so it suits ages 6 to 7 and up. A new family film, A Dog's Perfect Christmas, arrives on Netflix on November 4, 2026. We could not find a rating for it yet, and its story starts with a mother in a coma, so read the rating and preview it before younger kids watch.

What are the best Christmas movies for kids on Disney+?

The Muppet Christmas Carol (G, ages 5 and up) is on Disney+ as of October 19, 2026. The Nightmare Before Christmas is also there, but it is spooky enough that we cover it in our Halloween guide. Diary of a Wimpy Kid Christmas: The Getaway is scheduled for Disney+ on December 4, 2026, and its rating had not been announced when we checked.

What are the best animated Christmas movies for kids?

A Charlie Brown Christmas (ages 3 and up), The Grinch (2018, ages 5 and up), Arthur Christmas (ages 6 and up), Klaus and The Polar Express (ages 6 to 7 and up) are all animated.

What are the least scary Christmas movies for young kids?

A Charlie Brown Christmas is rated G, runs about 25 minutes and has nothing frightening in it. The Grinch (2018) is mostly slapstick, with a sad backstory for the Grinch. Both are good picks for ages 3 to 5.

What are the new Christmas movies for kids in 2026?

A Dog's Perfect Christmas (Netflix, November 4) and Diary of a Wimpy Kid Christmas: The Getaway (Disney+, December 4) are the two family films we found with set dates. Both are still unrated by us, so use the reviews and your own preview.
