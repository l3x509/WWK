What to reach out about

  • Feature requests. Want a new filter, a platform we don't support yet, or a different age range? Tell us — real parent feedback shapes what we build next.
  • Bug reports. If a title looks mis-rated, a link is broken, or something isn't loading, let us know what you saw and which device/browser you were using so we can track it down.
  • Content concerns. If you spot a recommendation that seems inappropriate for the age it was shown under, please flag it. We rely on data from TMDb, which isn't perfect, and your eyes help us catch the gaps.
  • Partnerships and press. If you write about family media or run a parenting community, we're happy to talk.

Response time

WatchWithKids is run by a small team — really, a very small team — so it may take a few days to get back to you. We read everything, and thoughtful feedback genuinely moves up the priority list.

Stay in the loop

If you'd like one carefully chosen family movie or show in your inbox each Friday — picked for your kids' ages, no scrolling required — you can subscribe to the newsletter from the home page.
