Find what to watch with your kids — in seconds

WatchWithKids is a free family movie and TV show finder for parents of children ages 3 to 12. Instead of scrolling endlessly through Netflix or Disney+, just set your kids' ages, pick a mood, and get a curated list of age-appropriate picks — filtered to your streaming platforms. Use the Avoid filter to skip scary scenes, violence, crude humor, or strong language.

Every result includes a Parent Enjoyment score so you can find movies and shows the whole family genuinely enjoys — not just content that keeps the kids quiet.

Frequently asked questions

What age range does WatchWithKids cover?

Ages 3 through 12. You can add up to 4 kids at different ages and the tool finds titles that work for all of them at once — great for families with a wide age gap.

Which streaming platforms are supported?

Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, Apple TV+, HBO Max, Hulu, Peacock, and Paramount+. Select "All" to see every available title regardless of platform.

What is Parent Enjoyment Mode?

Toggle it on to filter results to highly-rated titles that parents genuinely enjoy too — not just kids' content you'll have to sit through. Think Pixar, Studio Ghibli, and the best animated films ever made.

Is WatchWithKids free?

Completely free. No account, no sign-up. Just set your filters and find something to watch.

How do I find a short show for bedtime?

Select 😌 Calm under mood and Shows under type. You'll get calm episodes perfect for winding down before bed.
