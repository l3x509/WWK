New Family Movies & Shows This Month: October 2026

🍿 WatchWithKids Updated October 8, 2026 6 min read

October is Halloween month, so most of what's new for families leans spooky. The trick is sorting the gently spooky from the genuinely scary. This page is refreshed every month, and everything below is sorted by how it will land with different ages.

Titles come from streaming schedules published in early October 2026. Catalogs change and can differ by country, so check the service before you plan a movie night. Age notes are our own judgment. Gently spooky: good for younger kids

These have a Halloween feel without real scares. They are the safer picks for ages 4–8 and for mixed-age groups.

Hotel Transylvania & Hotel Transylvania 2
Paramount+ and Hulu · PG · added October 1

Monsters run a hotel and a human walks in. It's slapstick and silly more than scary, which makes it the easy choice for a first Halloween movie night.

Horton Hears a Who!
Netflix · G · added October 1

No Halloween theme, but a warm, brightly colored pick if you want something calm and friendly this month.

Dr. Seuss' The Grinch (2018)
Peacock · PG · October 5

A grumpy green recluse and a small town. It reads as seasonal rather than scary and works for the whole family.

Kiff and Phineas and Ferb Halloween specials
Disney+ · October 7

Short Halloween episodes, ideal for a weeknight when a full movie is too much. Vampirina: Teenage Vampire Season 2 also arrives on Disney+ the same day.

Kung Fu Panda 1–3
Prime Video · PG

Action-comedy with cartoon fights and no real horror. A good fit for kids who want something energetic rather than spooky.

Properly spooky: best for ages 8+

These are well made and loved by many kids, but they have unsettling imagery that can stick with younger children. Preview them first if your child scares easily.

Coraline, ParaNorman, The Boxtrolls, Kubo and the Two Strings
HBO Max and Peacock · PG · added October 1

A stop-motion run from Laika, with Corpse Bride also on HBO Max. They are creative and atmospheric, with creepy moments and real emotional weight. Good for confident 8-year-olds and up.

Monster House, The Spiderwick Chronicles, Hook
Peacock · PG · October 1 catalog

Monster House is the scariest of this group. Hook and The Spiderwick Chronicles are adventure first, with some tense scenes.

Not for young kids

A few popular October arrivals are aimed at older audiences. The Hunger Games films (Disney+ and Hulu, October 14) are rated PG-13 and deal with violence, so they suit teens, and are best previewed by a parent for anyone younger. Several horror titles also arrive this month on Prime Video, Peacock and HBO Max. Those are adult titles, not family viewing.

FAQ

What are the best Halloween movies for young kids?

Start with the gentle options: Hotel Transylvania, The Grinch (2018) and the Halloween specials on Disney+. Save Coraline, ParaNorman and Corpse Bride for kids around 8 and older, because their imagery is genuinely creepy.

Is Coraline okay for a 7-year-old?

It's rated PG, but it's one of the scariest family films on this list. Many parents hold it until about age 8 or older, and preview it for kids who get frightened easily.

How often is this page updated?

Once a month, around the start of the month, with the date at the top. If a title has moved services, we correct it.

Why does a title I see here not appear on my streaming service?

Streaming catalogs differ by country and change often. Use the platform filter on WatchWithKids for current availability in your region, and check the service before you start the movie.
